About This Project

Cybersecurity awareness demonstration · build v2.4

Important Notice

This application is an educational simulation. It is a front-end demonstration only and does not perform any real surveillance, monitoring, tracking, phishing, or device access. Every value displayed is fictional and generated locally in your browser.

Monitoring or accessing another person's device, messages, or location without their informed consent is illegal in most jurisdictions and is a serious criminal offence. This project exists to raise awareness of how such social-engineering and surveillance tactics can appear, so that people can recognise and avoid them.

What's included

  • Operator dashboard with a live console
  • Platform panels: WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Telegram, Snapchat
  • Location tracking, parental controls, app usage, notification mirror, camera & gallery
  • Phishing-link and payload generators (produce placeholder text only)

All generated "links" and "files" contain placeholder text. No executable or malicious content is ever produced.

Data & privacy

Nothing leaves your device. The demo stores generated items in your browser's local storage only and makes no network requests. Clearing site data removes everything.
